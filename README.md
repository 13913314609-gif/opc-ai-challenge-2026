# Talent Gathering AI · Future Enlightening Practical Challenge
才聚 AI·智启未来 实战挑战赛

![Poster](./assets/才聚AI挑战赛.jpg)

> Official repository for the "Talent Gathering AI · Future Enlightening Practical Challenge", co-hosted by China (Nanjing) Software Valley and Huawei Cloud.
> This competition aims to discover outstanding AI talents, focusing on real industrial demands — not just pitching, but landing real business orders.

## 📅 Timeline
- **Registration**: Open now
- **Preliminary Round**: November 2026
- **Industrial Matching & Review**: December 2026
- **Final Demo & Award Ceremony**: January 2027

## 🎯 Two Tracks
### Track 1: OPC Group (Individual / Early Team)
For university teams, independent developers, researchers and early founders.
Team size ≤ 5. Works can be prototype, demo or launched products.

### Track 2: Startup Group (Registered Enterprise)
For registered AI companies with proprietary technology, released products and real users or revenue.

A total of 40 teams (20 per track) will advance to the grand final.

- Talent policy consultation and incubation services
